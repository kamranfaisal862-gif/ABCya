Questions

Is this a real product?

No — ABCya here is a free demo website made for practice.

Does it cost anything?

No. The demo is free and has no payments.

Which ages is it for?

The demo is written with ages 5–10 in mind.

Why does the speaker button sometimes stay quiet?

It uses your device's built-in voice. Some devices have it turned off or muted.
